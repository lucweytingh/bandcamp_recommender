# Diversity strategy side-by-side — results

_Generated by `scripts/run_diversity_eval.py`. n_seeds=150 per regime, base_seed=20260616. Reproducible: rerun to reproduce exactly._

**Framing — constrained optimization.** The user's ask is *maximize diversity subject to the vibe staying similar and the music staying related to the seed*. So vibe (intensity band) and realm (seed-distance) are **constraints** enforced by the gate, not objectives to trade against diversity. Quality metrics below are computed over the **feasible** slices only (those that keep the vibe), so variants are compared on equal footing.

Legend — **vibe✓** fraction of slices that keep the seed's vibe (constraint satisfaction; higher better). **fill** fraction that filled k. Objective: **div↑** continuous style diversity (≈ what mmr/maxmin optimize), **gcov↑** distinct genres/slice, **acov↑** distinct artists/slice (gcov/acov are a *distinct categorical* measure — correlated with the continuous objective via the corpus's genre≈style structure, not identical to it; see §6). Constraint context: **disp** intensity dispersion within the slice (stays under the band by construction), **rel** mean seed-distance. **Q** the composite heuristic (context only — see §5). **gap↓** below the (approximate beam) oracle.

_Note: the headline comparison is **baseline_gated vs the strategies** — all four select from the identical gated pool and average over the same feasible slices. The ungated **baseline** row is context only; its quality cells average over just its in-vibe subset (survivorship-filtered), so they aren't directly comparable — read its **vibe✓** as the real story there._

## 5. Does the verdict survive a distinct (categorical) metric?

Q is ~dominated by its diversity term, which is close to what mmr/maxmin optimize, so ranking by Q alone would be partly self-referential. We therefore also rank by a *categorical* measure — distinct-genre coverage, which no strategy maximizes as a count. **Caveat (be honest):** this is a *distinct* function, not an *independent* one — in this corpus genre is the latent style cluster, so genre coverage is correlated with the continuous style objective (§6 quantifies how that correlation weakens as genre decouples from style). A set can still raise mean-pairwise style-distance without adding a genre (two far-apart members of two genres), so the two measures are not identical — but treat coverage as *correlated corroboration*, not a clean independent witness.

- Rank by **Q**: oracle > maxmin > mmr > stratified > baseline_gated > baseline
- Rank by **genre-coverage**: stratified > oracle > maxmin > mmr > baseline_gated > baseline
- baseline_gated genre-coverage = 1.093; mmr=1.751, maxmin=1.851, stratified=1.981 — all above the gated top-k baseline (in the realistic genre≈style regime).

## 6. How independent is genre coverage? (genre↔style coupling)

If genre encodes style (a coherent collection), the categorical coverage metric corroborates the continuous one. As the within-genre jitter (style_std) grows, genre stops predicting style, and the coverage corroboration for the *style*-optimizers (mmr/maxmin) should fade — while the continuous diversity win persists, and stratified (which targets the genre count itself) keeps its coverage edge. Gaps are vs baseline_gated on the diffuse_coupled_manygenre regime.

| 0.12 | +1.420 | +1.640 | +1.813 | +0.373 | +0.420 |
| 0.25 | +1.347 | +1.600 | +2.173 | +0.440 | +0.520 |

Reading: mmr/maxmin's **Δgcov shrinks toward 0** as style_std rises (their coverage gain was genre-mediated), but their **Δdiv stays clearly positive** (the continuous spread win is real and regime-robust), and stratified's Δgcov stays largest (its win is intrinsic to its objective). So the honest, regime-independent claim is the *continuous* diversity win; coverage corroborates it only where genre tracks style.

## 8. Conclusion

- **The gate is what keeps the vibe.** Ungated baseline keeps vibe 94% of the time; every gated variant is ~100%. The intensity-similarity requirement is met by the hard gate, independent of which strategy runs.
- **All three strategies increase the continuous style diversity they target over the gated top-k baseline** (div 0.19 → mmr 0.41 / maxmin 0.44 / stratified 0.38) — the robust, regime-independent result. Distinct-genre coverage corroborates it in the realistic genre≈style regime (1.09 → 1.75 / 1.85 / 1.98), but that's *correlated* corroboration, not independent (§5–§6): as genre decouples from style the coverage gain for mmr/maxmin fades while their continuous win holds.
- **Trade-off:** maxmin maximizes raw spread (div 0.44) but drifts furthest from the seed (rel 0.16) and runs slightly looser on vibe (disp 0.045). mmr gives nearly as much diversity (div 0.41) while staying most anchored (rel 0.14) and tightest on vibe (disp 0.040). stratified maximizes genre/artist coverage but depends on genre labels, which are sparse/noisy on real Bandcamp tags.
- **Decision: MMR (lambda≈0.5) for production.** It honors the user's stated priority order — vibe-similar first (tightest dispersion, most anchored), then maximally diverse within that — exposes a single knob (lambda) spanning baseline→pure-spread, degrades gracefully when genre tags are missing (unlike stratified), and sits within a hair of the oracle. maxmin is the drop-in alternative when raw spread matters more than staying close to the seed (set lambda lower instead).
